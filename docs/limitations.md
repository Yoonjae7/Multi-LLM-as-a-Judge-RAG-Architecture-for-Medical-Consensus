# Limitations

- This is a research prototype, not a diagnosis or prescription system.
- The Western medicine evidence library is small and supports only a limited set of common questions.
- Evidence summaries and source metadata require qualified clinical and bibliographic review before publication.
- Lexical retrieval thresholds and the confidence formula are experimental.
- The deterministic emergency screen cannot cover every phrasing or urgent presentation.
- The frontend and serverless functions form a research demo, not a production medical product.
- Syntax and integration tests do not establish clinical correctness, safety, or efficacy.
